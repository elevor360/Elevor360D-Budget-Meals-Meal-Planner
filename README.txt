Elevor360D Budget Meals & Meal Planner V1
=========================================
50 distinct budget-minded recipes with dish-specific methods; weekly meal planner,
shopping list, favourites, large graphic timers, personal recipes and backup.
New weekly food budget tracker: enter your own weekly budget and actual spending,
view remaining amount, and export/import budget entries separately.

The Recipe Manager HTML should be kept on your computer, not uploaded to GitHub.
Use it to import recipes.json, edit recipes and export a new recipes.json.
Upload the other files and advertising folder to GitHub Pages repository root.
Existing advertising/adverts.json and Sunset Banner.png included.
For recipe updates, increment CACHE in sw.js before deployment so installed
users receive the new data when they next open online.

The 50 recipes are editorial drafts and have not been individually kitchen-tested.
No claims of current supermarket prices or guaranteed meal costs are made.
Check allergens and safe handling, especially for poultry, eggs and leftovers.
